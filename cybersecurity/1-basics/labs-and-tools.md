# Labs and Tools

**Safety:** only attack systems you own or have written permission to test. Keep vulnerable VMs and malware on a host-only or isolated network with no shared folders, and snapshot before every session.

**Path at a glance:** VirtualBox with snapshots → Kali → one guided platform (TryHackMe) → add Hack The Box and specialty tools as you choose a path.

| Resource | Level | Cost | What it is | You will be able to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [VirtualBox documentation](https://www.virtualbox.org/wiki/Documentation) | Beginner | Free | Official docs for the free hypervisor. | Create VMs, host-only networks and snapshots for safe, repeatable labs. |
| [Kali Linux Documentation](https://www.kali.org/docs/) | Beginner | Free | Official docs for the standard offensive distro. | Install Kali in a VM and find your way around its tools. |
| [TryHackMe](https://tryhackme.com) | Beginner | Free tier + paid | Guided browser labs with an in-browser attack box. | Practice from fundamentals through offensive and defensive paths. |
| [Hack The Box](https://www.hackthebox.com) | Beginner to Intermediate | Free tier + paid | Starting Point machines and Academy modules. | Practice pentest methodology on realistic machines. |
| [Containerlab](https://containerlab.dev) | Intermediate | Free | Supplementary: deploys topologies from YAML using Docker. Also listed in the [networking labs](../../networking/1-basics/labs-and-tools.md). | Build repeatable multi-host lab networks to attack and defend. |
| [pwn.college](https://pwn.college) | Beginner to Advanced | Free | ASU-backed platform focused on low-level security: binary exploitation, reverse engineering, kernel, and more. Challenges are structured into "dojos" with progressive difficulty. | Master core offensive security skills through hands-on, ctf-style challenges. |

**pwn.college resources:**

| Resource | What it is |
|---|---|
| [pwn.college](https://pwn.college) | Official platform — start here. Create a free account and pick a dojo. |
