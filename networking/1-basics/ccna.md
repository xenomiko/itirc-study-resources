# CCNA

The most recognized entry-level networking cert. Verify the current exam version on Cisco's site before booking.

| Resource | Type | Level | Free/Paid | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Jeremy's IT Lab CCNA](https://www.youtube.com/@JeremysITLab) | YouTube course | Intermediate | Free | Full course with labs and Anki decks. Best free option. |
| [David Bombal CCNA content](https://www.youtube.com/@davidbombal) | YouTube | Intermediate | Free | Large library, good for second explanations and career advice. |
| [Neil Anderson CCNA (Udemy)](https://www.udemy.com) | Course | Intermediate | Paid | Cheap on sales, lab heavy. |
| [Boson ExSim-Max](https://www.boson.com) | Practice exams | Intermediate | Paid | The standard for exam prep, with detailed explanations. |
| [subnettingpractice.com](https://subnettingpractice.com) | Website | Intermediate | Free | Drill subnetting until it's automatic. |
| [Cisco U.](https://u.cisco.com) | Official platform | Intermediate | Free tier + paid | Cisco's own learning platform. |
