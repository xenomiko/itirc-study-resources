# Books and Deeper Theory

| Resource | Type | Level | Free/Paid | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kurose & Ross, *Computer Networking: A Top-Down Approach* | Textbook | Intermediate | Paid | Standard university text. The author's lecture videos are free online. |
| [Beej's Guide to Network Programming](https://beej.us/guide/bgnet/) | Online book | Intermediate | Free | The classic intro to socket programming in C. |
| [High Performance Browser Networking](https://hpbn.co) | Online book | Advanced | Free | How TCP, TLS, and HTTP/2 behave in practice. |
| Stevens & Fall, *TCP/IP Illustrated, Vol. 1* | Book | Advanced | Paid | The definitive deep dive. Save it for later. |
