# Labs and Tools

| Resource | Type | Level | Free/Paid | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cisco Packet Tracer | Simulator | Beginner | Free | Free via a Networking Academy account. Start here. |
| [Wireshark](https://www.wireshark.org) | Tool | Beginner | Free | Packet analysis is core for networking and security. |
| [Chris Greer](https://www.youtube.com/@ChrisGreer) | YouTube | Intermediate | Free | The best Wireshark and troubleshooting tutorials. |
| [GNS3](https://www.gns3.com) | Emulator | Advanced | Free | Realistic topologies with real device images. |
| [Containerlab](https://containerlab.dev) | Lab tool | Advanced | Free | Lightweight and code-driven. Runs well on Fedora. |
| [TryHackMe](https://tryhackme.com) | Guided labs | Beginner | Free tier + paid | Networking rooms that lead into cybersecurity. |
