# Fundamentals

**Path at a glance:** Skills for All (structure and a first lab) → Professor Messer (full syllabus) → Practical Networking (subnetting, VLANs, NAT).

| Resource | Level | Cost | What it is | You will be able to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Cisco Skills for All](https://www.skillsforall.com) | Beginner | Free | Cisco Networking Academy's free self-paced courses, including networking essentials and a Packet Tracer intro, with badges. | Explain how data moves across a network and build a first simulated topology. |
| [Professor Messer Network+](https://www.professormesser.com) | Beginner | Free | Complete vendor-neutral video syllabus on YouTube. | Cover every Network+ objective and use the standard vocabulary. |
| [Practical Networking](https://www.practicalnetworking.net) | Beginner | Free | Short articles and videos with clear diagrams. | Subnet confidently and explain VLANs, NAT and routing. |
| [Cloudflare Learning Center](https://www.cloudflare.com/learning/) | Beginner | Free | Concept primers written for newcomers. | Explain DNS, TCP, TLS and DDoS at a conceptual level. |
| [NetworkChuck](https://www.youtube.com/@NetworkChuck) | Beginner | Free | Motivational YouTube intros. Good for orientation, not depth. | Get oriented and decide what to study next. |
